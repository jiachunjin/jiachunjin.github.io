About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), working with Prof. Zhijie Deng. I am currently interning at Sharpa.

My research focuses on multimodal generative models and representation learning. I am currently interested in how to design multimodal models from first principles so that language and other perceptual modalities work together more effectively to advance multimodal intelligence.
